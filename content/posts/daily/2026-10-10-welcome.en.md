Want to know what has been happening lately at frontiers such as quantum computing, spaceflight and AI safety, without digging through papers, press releases and social media yourself? The daily digest here picks out the news worth noticing and explains it in plain language.

Compared with an ordinary news roundup, it differs in four ways:

- **Primary sources**: items come from papers, institutional press releases and public posts by the parties involved, and the paraphrase does not go beyond the original.
- **Context filled in**: each item first explains what happened and who is involved, and explains technical terms the first time they appear.
- **One framework for the reading**: every daily has a "Unified Systems View reading" section that says which field each item belongs to and why it is worth noticing; when there is too little information, it says so rather than forcing a judgement.
- **Every item can be checked**: statements carry numbers that match the links to original sources at the end.
