A few minutes a day to understand the new developments worth noticing at the frontier of science and technology.

Each daily digest picks the day's news from public primary sources, currently mainly in quantum computing, spaceflight and AI safety. Every item explains in plain language what happened, who is involved and why it is worth noticing, and explains technical terms along the way.

Every claim links to its original source and does not go beyond it. The raw material is archived in [markjin-data](https://github.com/markjin-lab/markjin-data), so you can check it at any time.
