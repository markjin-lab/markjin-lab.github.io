Understand a little more each day; see the bigger picture over time. This section starts from a checkable event and walks **macro → industry → company → reverse feedback**: the wider setting, then the industry, then the company, and finally whether those changes feed back into the earlier layers. Reporting period, publication date and primary sources stay in the text; inferences and hypotheticals are labeled.

Short portrait videos help explanation; the article carries the full evidence. For economic learning only; **not investment advice**.
