Unified Systems View: cognitive practice and self-evolution with AI assistance

The Unified Systems View is a common structural language that applies across fields. It sees complex systems in the economy, stock markets, the human body, and even the physical world as sharing the same kind of structure: this structure recurs across levels, and a change at one level propagates to other levels. It uses the same set of steps to observe and analyze these systems. What it unifies is the language for looking at problems, not the facts of each field.
