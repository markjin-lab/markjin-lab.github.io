Unified Systems View: cognitive practice and self-evolution with AI assistance

The Unified Systems View is a structural language shared across fields. It treats complex systems in the economy, the stock market, the human body and even the physical world as one and the same fractal structure: a structure that recurs at levels of different sizes, where a change at one level is transmitted to the other levels. It then uses the same set of steps to observe and diagnose these systems. What it unifies is the language for looking at problems, not the facts of each field.
