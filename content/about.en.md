Unified Systems View: cognitive practice and self-evolution with AI assistance

The Unified Systems View is a common way to describe the structure of complex systems across fields. It sees the economy, stock markets, the human body and the physical world as sharing the same recurring pattern. The pattern appears at different scales, and a change at one scale carries over to others. It uses the same steps to observe and analyze these systems. It offers a common language for looking at problems while keeping the facts of each field distinct.
